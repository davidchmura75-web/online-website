About
Me
ME + CS · robotics

I'm a mechanical and computer science student who builds robots and the systems that run them. Most of my work sits at the intersection of physical design and computation — where the math of structures meets the math of control.

I care about understanding systems all the way down: designing and machining the parts, laying out the PCB, writing the firmware, building the control loop, and shipping the software that lets a person interact with it. I find the boundary between disciplines more interesting than either side alone.

Outside of robotics I'm interested in dynamics simulation, mechanism design, and making tools that help other engineers work better.
